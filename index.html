Trainability: Measures how easily it is to train your dog breed

Score ranges from 1 - 5 where 1 = your dog is very difficult to train and 5 = your dog is very easy to train

Barking: Measures how vocal your dog breed is

Score ranges from 1 - 5 where 1 = minimal amount of barking and 5 = maximum amount of barking

Good With Strangers: Measures how compatible your dog breed generally is with strangers

Score ranges from 1 - 5 where 1 = least compatible with strangers and 5 = most compatible with strangers.

Max Life Expectancy: Measures the maximum life expectancy in calendar years.

Number indicates amount of expected calendar years NOT "dog years".
